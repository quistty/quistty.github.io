About Me

I’m a Mechanical Engineering student at McMaster University with a strong interest in robotics, mechanical design, automation, and product development. My interest in engineering started through smaller projects like building a Discord bot, where I learned programming, automation, debugging, and how to build systems that people could actually interact with. From there, I moved into more hands-on projects, including a dancing robot and school engineering competitions, which helped me become more interested in robotics and the connection between software, electronics, and mechanical design.

Today, I’m focused on designing more complex systems that bring those areas together. My current major project is a custom 6-DOF robotic arm that I’m designing from the ground up. The project has involved CAD, planetary gearboxes, belt reductions, motor selection, bearings, tolerances, encoders, controls, and extensive 3D-printed prototyping.

I enjoy learning by building, testing, and improving. Whether I’m working on code, mechanical assemblies, or full robotic systems, I like breaking large problems into smaller challenges and figuring out how each part contributes to the final result. Each project gives me a chance to strengthen my technical skills while becoming a more capable and well-rounded engineer. Find out more.
