<p> W.I.P </p>

Designed and developed a custom 6-DOF robotic arm as a platform for learning mechanical design, robotics, embedded systems, and motion control. The project integrates custom planetary gearboxes, belt reductions, magnetic encoders, 3D-printed components, and motor-driven joints while targeting a 500 mm reach and a 2 kg continuous payload.

The arm is being developed from the ground up, with each joint designed, prototyped, tested, and refined to improve torque, positioning, manufacturability, and overall system performance.
