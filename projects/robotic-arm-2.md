This robotic arm project is one of the core examples of my work in mechanical engineering and automation. It brings together CAD design, motors, structural components, transmissions, controls, and iterative prototyping into a single system that is continually being refined.

The focus is on understanding how a robot can be designed and built as an integrated system rather than just a collection of parts. Through this project, I am learning how mechanical feasibility, electronics, software, and prototyping all work together in real engineering development.
