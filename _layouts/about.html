About Me

I’m a Mechanical Engineering student at McMaster University with a strong interest in robotics, mechanical design, and product development. Through projects like these, I’m developing practical experience with SolidWorks, additive manufacturing, embedded systems, and robotics while continuing to explore how mechanical, electrical, and software systems come together.
