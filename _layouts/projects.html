{% if project.name == 'Robotic Arm' %}

Designed and developed a custom 6-DOF robotic arm as a long-term robotics and mechanical design project. The arm combines custom planetary gearboxes, belt reductions, stepper motors, magnetic encoders, 3D-printed structural components, and full joint assemblies, with a target reach of roughly 500 mm and a 2 kg continuous payload. The project has become a platform for learning CAD, machine design, motor control, embedded systems, forward/reverse kinematics, manufacturing tolerances, and iterative prototyping.

{% elsif project.name == 'Wearable Proposal' %}

Worked as part of a team to reverse engineer the Apple Watch into a simplified wearable designed to assist individuals with Alzheimer’s disease. The final concept removed the screen and focused on voice control, stronger haptic feedback, LED alerts, simplified interaction, and longer battery life to improve usability and accessibility. The project also included user research, iterative design, a bill of materials, cost analysis, and MATLAB-based evaluation of reminder effectiveness and production costs.

{% elsif project.name == 'Discord Bot' %}

Developed a Discord bot that calculates the potential profit from selling items on an in-game marketplace using current margins. The project combined API/data handling, automated calculations, and Discord commands to give users quick financial information directly through the platform. Building the bot helped strengthen my experience with programming, debugging, automation, and designing software around a practical user need.

{% elsif project.name == 'Java Based Game' %}

Developed a console-based Java game focused on careers, relationships, personal development, and decision-making. The project was designed around giving players multiple choices that influence how their simulated life progresses, creating a condensed version of long-term life experiences. It gave me experience thinking about game logic, branching systems, player interaction, and how software can be structured around interconnected decisions and outcomes. This game maintains a similar concept to the game, BitLife

{% elsif project.name == 'Custom Keyboard' %}

The project gave me hands-on experience with PCB design and component placement while exposing me to the electrical side of product development alongside my mechanical engineering work.
